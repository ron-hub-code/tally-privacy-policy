Privacy Policy for Tally

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Publisher: Melyoratio

Summary

Tally is a counter app. It lets you create counters, assign labels, increase or decrease totals, reset totals, and store counter and preference values for use by the app.

Information stored

Tally stores counter labels, counter totals, and app preferences for the app's operation. These values are stored by the app using local device storage.

Tally does not require an account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, health information, or other personal information to operate.

Transmission and sharing

Tally does not transmit counter data, app preferences, or personal information to Melyoratio or to third parties. Tally does not provide an account service or remote synchronization.

Android device backup may copy application data according to the user's device, account, and operating-system backup settings. Any such backup is controlled by Android and the user's device/account settings, not by a Tally server. This policy does not describe Android's separate backup service terms.

Advertising and tracking

Tally does not display advertisements and does not include an advertising or tracking service. Tally does not use analytics or cross-app tracking.

Local deletion and retention

The user can delete or reset counters in the app, clear Tally's app storage, or uninstall Tally. These actions remove the corresponding local app data according to normal Android behavior.

If Android has already created a backup copy, deleting local app data does not by itself constitute a request to delete that backup copy. The user must manage Android backup and account settings separately under the applicable Android controls and terms.

Tally has no server-side account or server database from which a separate Tally deletion request can be processed.

Children

Tally is a general-purpose app and is not specifically directed to children. Tally does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Tally's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed practices are introduced.
